## 🚫 What Strategy Avoids

### Fundamentals Gate Effectiveness

**Blocks stocks with:**
- ROE < 15% AND profit growth < 15%
- Negative profit companies
- Missing fundamentals data

**Example blocks:**
- MOTILALOFS: ROE 14.4%, growth -25.6% (avoided +21% pump & dump)
- SUNPHARMA: ROE 13.8%, growth 5.3%

**Impact:** ~10-15% fewer trades, but higher quality (blocks potential losers).

---

## ⚠️ Known Limitations

1. **Survivorship Bias**
   - Uses current NIFTY 500 list for historical dates
   - Inflates results by ~1-2%

2. **5-Minute Sequencing**
   - Cannot reproduce order book depth
   - Assumes stop-first on bars hitting both levels

3. **Missing Intraday Data**
   - 90 symbol-days missing
   - Minor impact on results

4. **Point-in-Time Issue**
   - No strict point-in-time fundamentals
   - Real trading would have 60-day delay
